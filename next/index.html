28Where to go next

Sources outside this guide that are worth your time, grouped by topic, each with the reason it earns a place.

History and the lineage

  • Bill Brewster and Frank Broughton, Last Night a DJ Saved My Life - the standard history of the DJ; the 2022 edition adds women's role550.
  • Simon Reynolds, Energy Flash (US: Generation Ecstasy) - the rave-culture history from Detroit and Chicago onward, updated through US EDM551.
  • John Kane, The Last Seat in the House: The Story of Hanley Sound - the story of Hanley Sound and festival sound's founding engineer; we know it through local-history coverage, not a publisher's page8.
  • Dead Heads Newsletter #19 (December 1974) - the closest thing to a primary spec sheet for the Wall of Sound, written mid-tour by the band's own camp74.
  • Owsley Stanley Foundation - Bear's mixing philosophy and an archive of soundboard tapes you can actually hear76.
  • Water & Music on Daft Punk's pyramid - the best-sourced account of how it was built and what it did to the industry, with its designer interviewed95.
  • Alan Krueger, "Rockonomics" (Princeton, 2002) - the foundational economics of why tickets and shows inflated102.
  • Smithsonian, "What is the Mothership?" - the museum's own short explainer on the prop as artifact87.
  • NPR, "Remembering Larry Levan" - first-hand quotes on the Paradise Garage's sound and its DJ's EQ habits146.
  • Moog Foundation timeline - a clear, dated history of electronic instruments9.
  • Library of Congress on the phonograph and gramophone - careful, caveated dates from people who own the objects34.

Scenes and history

  • Tim Lawrence, Love Saves the Day (Duke University Press, 2003) - the definitive history of the Loft and New York disco, the source behind much of what is written about both23.
  • NYC LGBT Historic Sites Project, Paradise Garage - short and authoritative on who the club was for and what it meant13.
  • City of Chicago, The Warehouse landmark report (2023) - an official primary source with rare production detail on the room house was named after14.
  • David Katz on Duke Reid, Coxsone Dodd and King Tubby - careful sound system history, including the myths he corrects1920.
  • London Museum, origins of Notting Hill Carnival - how Caribbean sound system culture took root in London21.
  • Dan Sicko, Techno Rebels - the standard book history of Detroit techno15.
  • Graham St John, The Local Scenes and Global Culture of Psytrance (Routledge, 2010) - the academic standard on Goa and psytrance50.
  • The Cambridge Companion to Electronic Dance Music (2026) - current, global and multi-perspective, edited by Hillegonda Rietveld and Toby Young16.
  • Sisters with Transistors (2020) - Lisa Rovner's film on the women who pioneered electronic sound68.
  • female:pressure FACTS reports - the long-running count of gender balance on festival line-ups69.

The booth

  • RBMA Daily, "The Evolution of DJ Mixers" - a strong single read on mixer history and the rotary revival; the archive is no longer updated110.
  • Resident Advisor, "Industry Standards" - a 2013 series on the gear that defined the booth; part two covers the CDJ552.
  • Technics, History of the SL-1200 - the official illustrated history; read it knowing it is a brand source106.
  • Roland, "The 303 Story" - Roland's own account of the box that made acid house140.
  • Digital DJ Tips - a frequently updated practical source on gear, firmware and streaming compatibility138.

Networks and control

  • Deep Symmetry's DJ Link Packet Analysis - the most detailed public description we found of how CDJs talk to each other, built from packet captures170.
  • Beat Link Trigger User Guide - a practical troubleshooting guide for getting a laptop onto a CDJ network158.
  • AlphaTheta PRO DJ LINK Setup Guide - the manufacturer's own wiring and player-number instructions, with current limits161.
  • ESTA Technical Standards Program - the official home of DMX512-A, RDM, sACN and ACN, with current editions178.
  • John Huntington, Show Networks and Control Systems - the standard textbook on entertainment control across lighting, sound, machinery and pyro553.
  • Yamaha's Dante Network Design Guide - switch selection, QoS, EEE and IGMP explained in a way that applies to any real-time show network164.
  • ENTTEC on Art-Net versus sACN - a plain side-by-side comparison, from a vendor182.

Light and lasers

  • Richard Cadena, Automated Lighting - the standard text on moving lights, LEDs, optics and lighting networks554.
  • PLSN "Backstage History" - well-sourced trade history, including the Vari-Lite story198.
  • ETC's "Casual History of Moving Lights" - a short, readable timeline from the 1920s patents onward90.
  • FDA, Laser Light Shows - the official US variance and reporting process and forms227.
  • eCFR, 21 CFR Part 1040 - the current legal text, with its amendment history225.
  • ILDA's lasershowsafety.info - plain-language US law, worldwide audience-scanning rules, the incident database and LSO classes245.
  • ILDA Technical Standards - the file formats and connector specs244.
  • HSE INDG224 - the UK legal framing for display lasers236.

Video

  • ROE Visual's LED thesaurus - clear definitions of refresh, scan, bit depth, moiré, genlock, COB and GOB256.
  • Planar on scan ratio and banding - a clear vendor explanation of why cameras and phones see bands on LED walls257.
  • Resolume's manual - the practical VJ manual: mapping, NDI, Syphon and Spout, DMX, timecode266.
  • Derivative's ISAM case study - a detailed look inside a landmark mapped show273.
  • TPi magazine - the trade magazine for live production, with detailed production profiles of tours555.

Sound and power

  • WHO Global Standard for Safe Listening Venues and Events - the international benchmark for audience sound levels311.
  • Live DMA, Sound regulations in Europe - a country-by-country comparison of limits313.
  • HSE, Noise in the music and entertainment sectors - UK worker rules and the Sound Advice guidance315.
  • Precision Audio Services on cardioid sub arrays - a clear practical explanation of why and how subs are steered303.
  • ESTA's electrical power working group - the home of the entertainment GFCI recommended practice323.

Effects, staging and safety

  • NIST NCSTAR 2, The Station nightclub fire - the primary investigation that reshaped nightclub codes345.
  • MagicFX Stage Flame manual - a real-world example of arm and fire channels, tilt sensors, emergency stops and danger zones332.
  • Mesa Fire on cold spark devices - a fire authority's plain-language case that "cold spark" is still pyro341.
  • NIOSH Pocket Guide: Carbon dioxide - the exposure limits behind any CO2 safety note327.
  • Stageco's EDC 2014 reference and Insomniac's kineticFIELD build - rare builder- and promoter-sourced numbers on an electronic mainstage3561.
  • The Purple Guide - the UK's event safety guide, subscription-based459.
  • Event Safety Alliance - US guidance, standards and training449.

Paperwork, broadcast and rights

  • Musicians Institute on advancing, stage plots and load-in - a clear beginner's guide to the paperwork436.
  • ProSoundWeb on input lists and stage plots - what working engineers want from those documents445.
  • Twitch DJ Program announcement - the primary source on licensed DJ livestreaming terms419.
  • YouTube Help, Copyright issues with live streams - exactly how interruptions and allowlisting work422.
  • Pioneer DJ CDJ-3000 product page - the full firmware changelog, for booth-readiness checks410.
  • AlphaTheta's KUVO / DJ Monitor notice - the current state of club track identification for royalties433.
  • PRS on setlist reporting - why setlists drive live royalties, with figures427.
  • Ableton Live 12 Manual, Session View - the reference for clip-based live performance403.

Show control and new technology

  • disguise Transports overview - how a media server chases LTC or MTC and takes cues285.
  • PLSN on Illenium's Ascend tour - a documented timecoded tour by an electronic dance music act, with its full crew list288.
  • PLSN on Swedish House Mafia's Paradise Again - a live-operated arena electronic dance music production in detail291.
  • FAA Part 107 waivers - the US rules a drone show has to waive370.
  • UK CAA Specific category overview - when swarms and near-crowd flights need authorization371.
  • ControlGeek, "It's Not a F*cking Hologram Part II" - the plain physics of Pepper's ghost versus holography382.
  • What Hi-Fi? on Dolby Atmos at Ministry of Sound - how object-based club audio actually worked for DJs372.
  • Gough Lui's PixMob wristband teardown - an independent look inside crowd LED bands377.

Money

  • Justin M. Jacobson on live performance agreements (Hypebot, parts 1 and 2) - an attorney's plain-English tour of fees, deposits, settlement, riders and radius clauses463468.
  • Prism.fm's concert budget guide - how a promoter builds a budget and a settlement; a vendor guide, so check the math467.
  • DJ Mag on the IMS Business Report 2026 - the headline numbers for the electronic music economy477.
  • Mixmag, "What DJs really earn" (2015) - dated and illustrative, but the rare tiered picture469.
  • Pollstar's Production Live! 2023 recap - production managers on post-pandemic costs and labor473.
  • FTC all-in ticket pricing rule - what US buyers should now see at checkout471.

Access and safety

  • Attitude is Everything, Live Events Access Charter - the UK's government-endorsed access standard for venues and festivals498.
  • ADA.gov ticketing guidance - the US rules on pricing, selling and holding wheelchair and companion seats495.
  • Epilepsy Society photosensitive epilepsy factsheet (2025) - flash-rate bands, the HSE club recommendation and the Ofcom TV rules in one place484.
  • The Loop - UK drug-checking charity, with its history and drug alerts507.
  • DanceSafe - US peer harm reduction, drug checking and consent education at events500.
  • Zendo Project - the psychedelic peer-support model and its principles for welfare teams511.
  • Good Night Out - accreditation and training on preventing sexual violence at venues and festivals513.

Sustainability

  • Powerful Thinking, The Show Must Go On (2015) - the baseline UK festival emissions report and the source of the "up to 80%" travel figure514.
  • A Greener Festival's 2023 carbon analysis - the updated breakdown that counts food properly516.
  • Tyndall Centre results for Massive Attack's ACT 1.5 - measured outcomes of a battery-powered, rail-first show for 32,000 people521.
  • Clean Scene, Last Night a DJ Took a Flight - the DJ-specific flight emissions study519.
  • Powerful Thinking's Glastonbury energy case study - how festival power is metered and right-sized525.

Careers

  • ETCP Rigging Candidate Handbook - the point tables, exam format, fees and renewal rules531.
  • PLASA National Rigging Certificate - UK rigger levels, assessment centers and costs533.
  • Audinate Dante Certification - the free three-level audio networking program534.
  • Music Support - the UK helpline and festival Safe Hubs for music workers545.
  • Mix on #RedAlertRESTART - the September 2020 crew protest and its demands548.
